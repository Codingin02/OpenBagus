The application uses an independently sourced, locally synthesized Indonesian TTS
voice with a gentle, calm, measured synthetic-robot demeanor, rather than bundling
extracted film dialogue, unauthorized sound recordings, or an unauthorized voice-cloning
model based on Scott Adsit's or any actor's performance.

## Critical IP & Safety Policies:

1. **No Original Film Recordings:** Zero dialogue clips, movie soundtracks, or sound effects
   from *Big Hero 6* are bundled, stored, or distributed within OpenBagus.
2. **No Actor Voice Cloning:** The application does not use unauthorized voice cloning datasets
   or attempt to synthesize an exact biometric impersonation of the original voice artist.
3. **Independent Speech Synthesis:** All voice output is synthesized at runtime on the local machine
   using open-source acoustic synthesis engines (Piper ONNX) trained on open speech datasets.

5. **Pre-Distribution Review:** Any future public release, distribution, or commercialization
